# Gemini Cat-Girl & Anime Assets (Sakura Nyan OS 2.0)

Mascot artwork based on the Gemini Cat-Girl multi-form character set provided by the user:
- `mascot-full.png` / `mascot-gemini.png`: Canonical Gemini Starlight cat-girl full-body sprite (default Control Center sidebar).
- `mascot-antigravity.png`: Antigravity Balloon Gemini form.
- `mascot-engineer.png`: Cyber Engineer Gemini form (System Ops / Containers).
- `mascot-obsidian.png`: Obsidian Prism Gemini form (Accounts & Security).
- `mascot-avatar.png` / `mascot-avatar-64.png` / `mascot-avatar-{gemini,antigravity,engineer,obsidian}.png`: Circular bust portraits for Eww bar launcher, Control Center header, and contextual Nyx/Gemini guide bubbles.
- `mascot-peek.png`, `mascot-thinking.png`, `mascot-sleepy.png`: Pose variants anchored to the canonical Gemini cat-girl (header peek, AI sidebar thinking, power menu & lockscreen sleepy).
- `lock-bg.png` / `lock-bg-light.png`: Zero-banding eye-care dark & light Swaylock wallpapers.
- `*.svg` & `*-symbolic.svg`: Anime sticker and symbolic icon accents (`paw`, `sakura`, `star-sparkle`, `heart`, `cat-ears`, `ribbon`, `cloud`).
